Day 1

Pass the campaign and keyword into Adapty

After a one-time SDK setup, the Meta campaign or Apple Ads keyword of each install arrives as a user attribute and picks the flow at first open. No app release.

Week 1

One version per source

The primary care ad, the drug-check searches and the pill searches each get their own first screens; searches for your own name keep the default flow.

Week 2

Judge each source on what it earns

Each version against today’s first launch, measured on paid subscriptions and what each install from that source earns, so spend follows the sources that pay.

Most of your Meta ads (19 of 22) link to epocrates.com, not the app: they sell epocrates+ on the web or an article. In Apple Ads you show on 79% of searches for “epocrates: drug checker app” but only 2% for “epocrates”, so another app can take the top slot on your own name. Search data is account-level. Your epocrates+ ads offer $149.95 for the first year on epocrates.com, where the App Store lists $179.99 a year; the app never sees that offer. This layer carries no range: only a test per source can give one.
